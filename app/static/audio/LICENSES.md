## Acoustic Guitar Samples
The acoustic guitar samples (`E2.mp3`, `A2.mp3`, `D3.mp3`, `G3.mp3`, `B3.mp3`, `E4.mp3`) are sourced from the `midi-js-soundfonts` repository (FatBoy Soundfont converted to MP3).
- **Source:** [midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts)
- **Original Soundfont:** FatBoy
- **License:** CC0 (Public Domain) or equivalent open license depending on the soundfont distributor.

## Drum Samples
Drum samples (`kick`, `snare`, `hihat`, `hihat-open`, `ride`) are intended to be user-supplied or sourced from CC0 directories such as FreeSound.org. 
- **Recommendation:** Use CC0 samples to ensure you can distribute the application freely without attribution issues.
