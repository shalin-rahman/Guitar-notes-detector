# Ahordian Audio Licenses

All bundled audio is fetched by `app/download_samples.py`. Two licences apply —
the drum pack **requires attribution**, so this file is a distribution
requirement, not documentation.

## Acoustic Guitar Samples — CC0

Two selectable tones, 19 MP3s each (`E2` … `C6`, roughly every 3 semitones),
one directory per tone under `guitar/`. Both come from soundfonts converted to
MP3 by the `midi-js-soundfonts` project.

| Directory | Tone | Soundfont | Source path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `guitar/steel/` | Steel-string (default) | MusyngKite | `MusyngKite/acoustic_guitar_steel-mp3/` |
| `guitar/nylon/` | Nylon / classical | FatBoy | `FatBoy/acoustic_guitar_nylon-mp3/` |

- **Source:** [midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts)
- **License:** CC0 (public domain). No attribution required; kept here for provenance.

## Drum Samples — CC-BY 3.0 (attribution required)

`kick-acoustic01.wav`, `snare-acoustic01.wav`, `hihat-acoustic01.wav`,
`hihat-open01.wav`, `ride-acoustic01.wav` are the `kick-01`, `snare-01`,
`hihat-closed`, `hihat-open` and `ride-01` samples from the **Pearl Master
Studio** acoustic kit, renamed to the paths `DrumSampler` expects. The audio is
unmodified.
